  are mmcblk0boot0/1 (spl_a/spl_b).
- dtbo_idx=18, lcd_id=ID416002, lcd_name=lcd_td4160_hx_mipi_hdp_x6525.
- androidboot.selinux=permissive on this stock build.
- modem=shutdown in the cmdline; SIM inserted / carrier: not recorded.
- The second rear lens reports as "fakeserial" and is decorative.
